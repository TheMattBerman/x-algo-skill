# Editorial patterns to test

These are editorial patterns, not algorithmic predictions. The published code does not provide ideal lengths, expected action probabilities, or a format guarantee.

## Useful original

State a specific observation, show enough context to make it useful, and include a source when making factual claims. This is a practical way to create material another person may choose to share or use.

## Clear framework

Name a problem, give a short sequence of useful steps, and distinguish your experience from a general claim. Do not use a question merely to manufacture replies.

## Evidence-backed point of view

Make the claim, state the evidence or limitation, and invite substantive correction only when you genuinely want it. Before publishing, review engagement-bait and negative-feedback risk as human judgments.

## Relationship reply

Respond with a concrete addition, correction, or resource. This can serve conversation goals, but replies have a documented OON constraint and should not be presented as a discovery tactic.
